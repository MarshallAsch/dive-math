# Migration guide

How to move the app-local dive math onto `@marshallasch/dive-math`. Each row is old symbol, new symbol, and any
behaviour change. Unless noted, the new function throws (`RangeError`/`TypeError`) on invalid input instead of
returning `null`, `NaN` or `Infinity`, and returns unrounded numbers. Gas mixes are `Gas` objects (`gas(fo2, fhe)`),
fractions rather than percentages. Pressure is gauge bar unless a name says absolute.

### Behaviour changes to watch for in tests

- **Real-gas top-up direction.** The old linear Z made real-gas O₂ top-ups come out richer than ideal. Under the
  virial model the opposite can happen (for example real 0.6021 vs ideal 0.6030 in the ported test). Relational
  expectations in the app ("real is richer than ideal") may flip.
- **Booster real vs ideal.** Agreement at low pressure is within 1% only below about 20 bar (about 1.1% at 30 bar).
- **`realPressureForIdealEquivalent`** throws `RangeError` on non-convergence.
- **Cylinder volumes:** LP85 13.0 L and LP95 15.0 L (were 12.9 / 14.9), and AL40 5.7 L (Luxfer spec).
